Privacy policy

For the Tripjot waitlist. Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains what we collect when you join the Tripjot waitlist, why, and how you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details. It covers this website and the waitlist only, not the Tripjot app.

Who we are

Tripjot is run by Andrii Storcheus, a sole trader based in Ukraine. For data protection law (UK GDPR), we are the "controller" of your details.

Contact: hellotripjot@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, if you give it.
  • What you told us you are: home carer, self-employed or other.
  • That you ticked the box to get emails, and when you signed up.
  • Which advert or link brought you here, if the link says so (for example "facebook").

We do not ask for your address, phone number, bank details or anything about your health or the people you care for. The tax calculator on our website works in your browser: the numbers you type in are not sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To email you about Tripjot: when it launches, and your founding member price.
  • To send information that fits you, for example different emails for home carers and self-employed people.
  • To see which adverts bring people to the waitlist.

We use your details because you agreed to it (your "consent"). You can change your mind at any time.

Who else handles your details

We use a small number of services to run the website and send emails. They store your details for us and may only use them to provide their service to us:

  • Website hosting: Netlify, Inc. (USA)
  • Waitlist form: Netlify Forms (Netlify, Inc.)
  • Visitor statistics: Umami Cloud (Umami Software, Inc.)

We do not sell your details and we do not share them with your employer, your care agency or HMRC.

Because we are based in Ukraine, and some of these services may store data outside the UK, your details may be handled outside the UK. We only use services that protect personal data in line with UK GDPR.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until Tripjot launches and for up to 12 months after that, unless you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them sooner. Then we delete them.

Unsubscribe or delete your details

  • Every email from us has an "unsubscribe" link. One click and we stop emailing you.
  • To have all your details deleted, email hellotripjot@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my details". We will do it within one month and confirm by email.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • show you the details we hold about you;
  • correct them;
  • delete them;
  • stop using them;
  • give you a copy to take elsewhere.

Just email us. If you are not happy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

We count visits with Umami, a visitor statistics service that works without cookies. It shows us totals, for example how many people opened the page, used the calculator or joined the waitlist. It does not tell us who you are.

Children

The waitlist is for adults. Please do not sign up if you are under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change matters to you, we will email you.
