Part 1: The Tripjot app

In short: your trips stay on your iPhone. Tripjot has no servers and no accounts. The app has no analytics, no adverts and no trackers. We never see where you go.

What the app uses on your iPhone, and why

  • Location, set to "Always". So the app can notice when you start and finish a drive and record it, even when the app is closed.
  • Motion & Fitness. So the app can tell when you are in a car, ignore walks and save battery.

You can turn these off at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Without them, Tripjot cannot record trips by itself.

Where your data is kept

Your trips, places and reports are stored only on your iPhone. So are your report details (for example your name, employer and car registration): they are used only to print on your reports. None of this is sent to us.

If you back up your iPhone, the backup may include Tripjot's data, like any other app. That backup is controlled by you and Apple.

Apple services

To show street names and postcodes, the app asks Apple's address service. Sometimes it also asks Apple Maps for the route between two points. For these requests, the app sends the coordinates of those points to Apple. Apple handles them under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive them.

Subscriptions

Tripjot is a subscription that starts with a free trial. Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your card details. Apple tells us only sales totals that do not show who you are.

You can manage or cancel your subscription on your iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Using the app is covered by Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA).

Your reports

Reports (PDF or CSV) are made on your iPhone. You decide who to send them to, for example your accountant or HMRC.

When you send us feedback

"Send feedback" opens your own email app with a message to hellotripjot@gmail.com. We only get what you choose to send: your email address and your message.

You can also attach the app's log from the last 7 days. This is off unless you switch it on. The log shows when the app woke up and recorded trips, how long the trips were, and the battery level. It does not include locations, addresses or maps. We use it only to fix problems, and we delete it afterwards.

We use your email only to reply to you and fix the problem you told us about.

Deleting your data

Delete the Tripjot app, and all its data is removed from your iPhone. To have your feedback emails deleted, email us.

Tax

Tripjot's rates and totals are a record-keeping help, not tax advice. Tripjot is not part of HMRC and is not connected with it.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.

Part 2: This website and the waitlist

This part explains what we collect when you join the Tripjot waitlist, why, and how you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, if you give it.
  • What you told us about your work: employed, self-employed or both, and whether you do care work.
  • That you ticked the box to get emails, and when you signed up.
  • Which advert or link brought you here, if the link says so (for example "facebook").

We do not ask for your address, phone number, bank details or anything about your health or the people you care for. The tax calculator on our website works in your browser: the numbers you type in are not sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To email you about Tripjot: when it launches, and your founding member price.
  • To send information that fits you, for example different emails for employed and self-employed people.
  • To see which adverts bring people to the waitlist.

We use your details because you agreed to it (your "consent"). You can change your mind at any time.

Who else handles your details

We use a small number of services to run the website and send emails. They store your details for us and may only use them to provide their service to us:

  • Website hosting: Netlify, Inc. (USA)
  • Waitlist form: Netlify Forms (Netlify, Inc.)
  • Visitor statistics: Umami Cloud (Umami Software, Inc.)

We do not sell your details and we do not share them with your employer, your care agency or HMRC.

Because we are based in Ukraine, and some of these services may store data outside the UK, your details may be handled outside the UK. We only use services that protect personal data in line with UK GDPR.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until Tripjot launches and for up to 12 months after that, unless you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them sooner. Then we delete them.

Unsubscribe or delete your details

  • Every email from us has an "unsubscribe" link. One click and we stop emailing you.
  • To have all your details deleted, email hellotripjot@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my details". We will do it within one month and confirm by email.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

We count visits with Umami, a visitor statistics service that works without cookies. It shows us totals, for example how many people opened the page, used the calculator or joined the waitlist. It does not tell us who you are.

Children

The waitlist is for adults. Please do not sign up if you are under 16.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • show you the details we hold about you;
  • correct them;
  • delete them;
  • stop using them;
  • give you a copy to take elsewhere.

Just email hellotripjot@gmail.com. Your trips in the app never reach us, so we hold none of them. If you are not happy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change matters to you, we will tell you by email or in an app update.
