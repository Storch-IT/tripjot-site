Questions

Employed: my employer already pays mileage. Can I still claim?

Yes, if they pay less than 55p a mile, you can claim tax relief on the difference.

I'm self-employed. Is this for me?

Yes. If you use HMRC's mileage rate on your tax return, you need a record of your business miles. Tripjot keeps it for you.

Can you see where I go?

No. Your trips are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and we have no servers that receive your locations.
